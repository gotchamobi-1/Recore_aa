A DPF keeps requiring regeneration. What should I look at before replacing it?
Start by confirming whether soot loading is the cause or the symptom. Review differential pressure data, duty cycle, exhaust temperature behavior, sensor plausibility, injector/combustion issues and upstream faults before condemning the DPF. A repeated regeneration pattern often indicates an underlying condition rather than a failed filter.
